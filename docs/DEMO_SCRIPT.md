# Live Demo Script (3 minutes)

A complete walkthrough showing all five learning modes.

## 0:00 — 0:20 — Hook

> "When a student gets stuck at 11 PM, help arrives in hours — or never. Pratikriya answers instantly, and then goes further: it figures out _why_ the mistake happened."

Open the landing page. Ask: **"Explain the Pythagorean theorem with a simple example."**

Point out: step-by-step answer, diagram, key takeaways, "Listen to summary" voice button.

## 0:20 — 1:00 — Notes Intelligence

Go to **Notes AI**. Upload any PDF or paste lecture text.

Highlight the generated study pack: 3-tier summaries, flashcards, MCQs, mindmap, formula sheet, and the 7-day revision plan.

## 1:00 — 1:40 — Exam Mode

Go to **Exam Mode**. Upload source material, pick difficulty + question count, generate.

Answer a few questions, submit, and show the grading dashboard: score dial, accuracy, per-question feedback, strengths, mistakes, and **"Generate quiz on weak topics"**.

## 1:40 — 2:20 — AI Detective

Go to **AI Detective**. Enter a wrong answer (e.g. "(a+b)² = a² + b²") with a confidence level.

Walk through the suspect root causes, answer the probing questions, and reveal the verdict with the concept dependency tree and repair checklist.

## 2:20 — 3:00 — Reverse Teacher + Profile

Go to **Reverse Teacher**. Pick the _Skeptical_ student persona, explain a concept in two or three sentences (or use the whiteboard), and show the AI student's live notebook and the end-of-session report.

Finish on **Profile**: XP level, mastery dial, and the topic competency matrix that unifies everything the student did across all five modes.

> "Pratikriya doesn't just answer questions — it builds a complete picture of how you learn."
