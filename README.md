🚀 Pratikriya AI

An AI-powered personalized learning platform that helps students ask doubts, understand concepts, learn from mistakes, transform notes into study resources, and master topics through active learning.

Pratikriya AI is more than a question-answering chatbot. It combines an AI Tutor, Personalized Exam Generator, Notes Intelligence System, Reverse Teacher Mode, and AI Detective into one learning ecosystem.

Students can ask questions in their own words, upload study materials, generate personalized exams, teach concepts to an AI student, and investigate the root cause behind their mistakes.

 The Problem — Learning Shouldn't Wait

Students often encounter doubts while studying but may need to wait hours or even days for clarification.

By the time feedback arrives:
 
  Learning momentum may be lost
  
  -The original context may be forgotten
  
  -Confusion may become deeper
  
  -Students may stop asking questions

Pratikriya addresses this problem by providing instant and accessible academic assistance.

How Pratikriya Helps

Pratikriya is designed around three principles:

⚡ Instant, Not Eventual

Students receive explanations when the question arises instead of waiting for external feedback.

🎯 Personalized to You

The learning experience can adapt explanations, examples, and depth based on the student's needs.

❤️ Kind by Default

Students can ask questions freely in a judgment-free learning environment.

Reverse Teacher Mode — Learn by Teaching

The best way to understand something is to explain it.

Reverse Teacher Mode flips the traditional learning model.

Instead of the AI teaching the student:

Student → Teaches → AI Student

The AI can behave as different types of students:

AI Student	Behavior

🤔 Curious Student	Asks questions and wants real-world examples

🌱 Weak Student	Needs simple explanations and analogies

🎯 Exam Student	Focuses on marks, shortcuts, and memory

🧠 Smart Student	Learns quickly and challenges explanations

🔬 Research Student	Asks deeper questions about proofs and limitations

This method encourages:

Active recall
Deeper understanding
Clear communication
Identification of knowledge gaps

AI Detective — Investigate Your Mistakes

Every mistake is a clue.

AI Detective goes beyond simply marking an answer as wrong.

Students provide:

The question

Their answer

The correct answer, if available

Their confidence level

The AI then investigates the possible root cause
